Reading Club 2000 website images

Homepage image paths are configured near the top of public/home.php in $homeImages.
Put your files in these folders, or change the matching filename in that array:

  home_banner.jpg
  stories/story-reading.jpg
  stories/story-bookshelf.jpg
  stories/story-sharing.jpg
  stories/story-community.jpg
  articles/article-reading.jpg
  articles/article-sharing.jpg
  articles/article-community.jpg

Founder portrait (optional):
  circlephoto.jpg
  Its filename is configured near the top of public/about.php in $founderImage.

Use JPG, PNG, or WebP and keep the filename and extension in the PHP setting in sync.
The pages show designed color placeholders when an optional image is not present.
The logo used in the navigation and footer is public/assets/images/logo.png.
